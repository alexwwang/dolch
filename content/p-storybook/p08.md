{{< /sp >}}

{{< sp image="p/p08/page_02.jpg" page="2" >}}

{{< sp image="p/p08/page_03.jpg" page="3" >}}

{{< /sp >}}

{{< sp image="p/p08/page_04.jpg" page="4" >}}
"**This** is my white flower," said Sam. "**That** is your blue pebble," said Pip.
{{< /sp >}}

{{< /sp >}}

{{< sp image="p/p08/page_07.jpg" page="7" >}}
